File your own taxes (T2125)

Step 1
Get your annual summary
Uber/Lyft email a tax summary Jan/Feb. Self-employment — NOT a T4.
Step 2
Use free tax software
Wealthsimple Tax or StudioTax. Both CRA NETFILE certified.
Step 3
Add Form T2125
Industry code: 485310. Enter total income from Dashboard. Open the T2125 Helper page in Lucra for all values.
Step 4
Enter vehicle expenses
Use the CRA page for best method. Enter categories from P&L. Chart A values are auto-calculated in T2125 Helper.
Step 5
CCA for your car
Class 10 (≤$37K) or 10.1 (>$37K). 30% rate, half-year rule in first year. Area A values are in T2125 Helper.
Step 6
HST/GST
Register if you earn >$30,000 in 4 consecutive quarters. Once registered, collect 13% HST from Uber/Lyft.
Step 7
Keep records 6 years
Use Audit Vault + exports for all evidence. CRA can audit up to 4 years back.
