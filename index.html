File your own taxes (T2125)

Step 1
Get your annual summary
Uber/Lyft email you a tax summary in Jan/Feb. This is NOT a T4 — rideshare is self-employment.
Step 2
Use free tax software
Wealthsimple Tax (free) or StudioTax. Both are CRA NETFILE certified.
Step 3
Add Form T2125
Industry code: 485310 (Rideshare). Enter your total income from this app's Dashboard.
Step 4
Enter vehicle expenses
Use the CRA tab to see which method (mileage rate vs actual) gives bigger deduction. Enter all categories from P&L.
Step 5
CCA for your car
Class 10 or 10.1 (if >$38K). Software calculates it — enter purchase price and business start date.
Step 6
HST/GST
Register if income >$30K in 4 consecutive quarters. Below that it's optional.
Step 7
Keep records 6 years
CRA requires 6 years of records. Your Vault captures + data exports are your evidence. Back up regularly!
